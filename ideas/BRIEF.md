# Idea round: brief for every agent

## Event: FirstYearHack 2026 (KCL Tech), Sat 21 Nov 2026
- Build with JetBrains Air (required). Teams up to 3. All work today. Any language/framework/API.
- Draft submission 17:30, final 18:00, late entries not judged. Working demo must be frozen by ~15:30.
- Team: 2 first-year CS students, 6 AI agents. Build time ~5 hours.

## Tracks (pick one)
1. Creative Tech / Code Meets Culture: remix music, art, film, fashion or stories in new ways.
   Judged on: creative/wacky, connecting with culture, cool experiences.
   Examples: Spotify history into art; choose-your-own-adventure about freshers' week; outfit randomiser from wardrobe photos.
2. Retro Remix / Old Tech, New Tricks: take something old (game, gadget, website, trend) and rebuild it for 2026.
   Judged on: reinventive, surprising + faithful.
   Examples: multiplayer Snake over the web; 2000s-style social network for your uni flat; modern Tamagotchi in a browser tab.

## Side prize: best use of Snowflake
See docs/snowflake.md. Snowflake must do real AI/data work (Cortex AI functions, Cortex Search, Agents,
Streamlit in Snowflake, Marketplace data), not just storage.

## Free credits available: Softr (build web apps with AI, 200 free credits).

## What to write
Create ONLY your own file: ideas/<your-seat>.md (codex.md, argon.md, neon.md, claude66.md, asim.md).
Never edit another seat's file. Give 3 ideas, each with:
- Name + one-line pitch
- Track it targets and why it scores on that track's criteria
- How Snowflake is used (specific features)
- Demo moment: what judges see in the first 30 seconds
- Build plan for 5 hours with 2 people + agents: frontend / backend / data, and what could break
- Wow factor 1-10 and feasibility 1-10, honest
Avoid copying the slide examples directly. Be specific, not generic.
Then commit "Ideas: <seat>" and push.
